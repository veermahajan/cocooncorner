Our Curricula

Week-long lesson plans for classrooms — ready to teach, free to use.

Each curriculum is a five-day unit designed for elementary-age students. Lessons explore neurodiversity through movement, discussion, storytelling, and art. Click a grade level below to expand its full lesson plan. Download individual day worksheets or full-grade PDFs using the file links provided.

Please do not share these lesson plans without our consent

These curricula are free for you to use in your own classroom, but they may not be redistributed, republished, or shared beyond it without written consent from Cocoon Corner.

Interested in adopting these materials as part of an official school or district curriculum? We would love to hear from you — reach out at team@cocooncorner.org or gramkumar@cocooncorner.org to learn more.
