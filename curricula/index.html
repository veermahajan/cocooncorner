Our Curricula

Week-long lesson plans for classrooms — ready to teach, free to use.

Each curriculum is a five-day unit designed for elementary-age students. Lessons explore neurodiversity through movement, discussion, storytelling, and art. Click a grade level below to expand its full lesson plan and preview or download the PDF.

Written by people, not AI. We don’t use generative AI to create any lesson plan, worksheet, or teacher education content.

Please don’t share these without our consent. Free to use in your own classroom, but not to redistribute. For official school or district use, contact team@cocooncorner.org or gramkumar@cocooncorner.org.
